[ SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE ]

Zero Compromises. Air-Gapped by Design.

Every subsystem was architected from the ground up for strict isolation, impenetrable encryption, and high velocity.

Zero-Knowledge Hardware Vault

Your master password never touches unencrypted memory. Data is derived via Argon2id key stretching and encrypted with authenticated AES-256-GCM backed by Android's Hardware Keystore / Keymaster.

AES-256-GCM Argon2id Android Keystore

Air-Gapped 2FA Authenticator

Compliant with RFC 6238 (TOTP) and RFC 4226 (HOTP). Generates tokens completely offline with support for SHA-1, SHA-256, and SHA-512 algorithms, custom period lengths, and QR scanning.

RFC 6238 RFC 4226 100% Offline

Disposable Email & OTP Extraction

Spin up instant throwaway mailboxes to protect your real identity from marketing scrapers and credential stuffing. Features automated regex-driven OTP extraction directly to your clipboard.

Spam Shield Auto OTP Instant Destroy

Entropy Password Studio

Generate cryptographically secure passwords backed by high-entropy OS CSPRNG. Includes real-time Shannon entropy calculation, brute-force resistance projection, and Diceware wordlists.

CSPRNG Diceware Shannon Entropy

Cross-Platform Sovereign Sync

Import and export encrypted backups anytime in standardized formats. Seamlessly switch between the Android App, Chrome Extension, and Offline Desktop workstation without vendor lock-in.
