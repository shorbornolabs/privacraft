[ THREAT INTELLIGENCE ]

Why Sovereign Privacy is Mandatory

Conventional tools compromise privacy in insidious ways. Here is what PrivaCraft protects you against.

VECTOR 01: SEED INTERCEPTION

The Danger of Shady Web 2FA

Online generators like 2fa.live transmit secret seeds over remote HTTP endpoints. Anyone with server access can duplicate your authenticators permanently.

VECTOR 02: BIG TECH LOCK-IN

The Cloud Sync Trap

Mainstream authenticators link your 2FA seeds to a single monolithic corporate account. If that account is locked or SIM-swapped, you lose access to all your digital identity.

VECTOR 03: IDENTITY SCRAPING

Spam & Data Broker Tracking

Entering your personal email into signup forms links your real identity to thousands of marketing brokers. Disposable temp mail acts as an impermeable blast shield.

VECTOR 04: CREDENTIAL STUFFING

Weak Password Re-use

Over 80% of data breaches originate from reused or predictable passwords. Cryptographic entropy with CSPRNG ensures passwords resist trillion-guess supercomputing arrays.
