[ THREAT INTELLIGENCE ]

Why Sovereign Defense is Mandatory

Conventional tools compromise privacy in insidious ways. Here is what PrivaCraft protects you against.

VECTOR 01: SEED INTERCEPTION

The Danger of Shady Web 2FA

Online generators like 2fa.live transmit secret seeds over remote HTTP endpoints. Anyone with access to server logs or network sniffers can duplicate your authenticators permanently.

VECTOR 02: BIG TECH LOCK-IN

The Cloud Sync Trap

Mainstream authenticators link your 2FA seeds to a single monolithic corporate account. If that account is flagged, locked, or SIM-swapped, you lose access to all your digital infrastructure.

VECTOR 03: IDENTITY SCRAPING

Spam & Data Broker Tracking

Entering your personal email address into signup forms links your real identity to thousands of marketing brokers. Disposable temp mail acts as an impermeable blast shield.

VECTOR 04: CREDENTIAL STUFFING

Weak Password Re-use

Over 80% of data breaches originate from reused or predictable passwords. Cryptographic entropy with CSPRNG ensures passwords resist trillion-guess supercomputing arrays.

Zero-Knowledge Hardware Vault

Your master password never touches unencrypted memory. Data is derived via Argon2id key stretching and encrypted with authenticated AES-256-GCM backed by Android's Hardware Keystore / Keymaster.

Compliant with RFC 6238 (TOTP) and RFC 4226 (HOTP). Generates tokens completely offline with support for SHA-1, SHA-256, and SHA-512 algorithms, custom period lengths, and QR scanning.
